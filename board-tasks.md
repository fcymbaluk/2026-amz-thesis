## Task 4 — Incumbency survival exploration (label: exploratory)

Hypothesis (post-hoc): among listed municipalities, incumbents/incumbent parties survive post-listing elections better where BF coverage is high, because transfers cushion the livelihood shock enforcement imposes (Mechanism 3's most direct observable implication). Construct re-election/party continuity from `elec_winner_id` (99% populated) and `elec_winner_party` across the 2008/2012/2016 cycles; compare survival by coverage stratum among the 43 listed, benchmarked against never-listed at-risk municipalities. A triple-interaction ATT test was ruled out (2×2 cells of 10–11; MDE \> 1 SD). This survival test replaces it.

## Task 5 — Persistence exploration (label: exploratory)

Question: does social protection sustain compliance after enforcement pressure fades ("underwriting" in the strict sense)? Two designs:

-   **System-level (preferred):** the post-2016 weakening / post-2019 collapse as an all-at-once enforcement withdrawal; test whether the deforestation surge was flatter in high-coverage municipalities. Moderator stays the predetermined 2006–07 value — contemporaneous coverage is post-treatment (rank stability across windows is only ρ = 0.41) and must not be used as an updated moderator. Control for price exposure (surge coincides with commodity environment). Window capped at 2020 (BF identity changes in 2021; also the 2020 BF gap from Task 1.1).
-   **Municipality-level (descriptive at best):** post-exit relapse among the 18 exiters (14 from the 2008 cohort; exits concentrate 2012–13 and 2018; 4 municipalities were re-listed). Exit is selection on the outcome — only within-exiter comparisons by stratum are admissible.
-   Building blocks already exist: the B5 rebound measure (mean normalized deforestation 2013–16 minus 2008–12; 61% of at-risk municipalities show some rebound) and the C2 event-time tail. Possible side observation to check en route: coverage eroded in listed municipalities relative to controls post-listing (t ≈ −1.2; noise-level, earns a look not a claim).
